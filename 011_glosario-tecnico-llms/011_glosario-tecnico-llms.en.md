## Why download this glossary?

When you start building with AI, LLMs give you better answers when you can name what you want precisely.

You do not need to become a senior developer. But you do need to recognize words like `API`, `deploy`, `frontend`, `backend`, `token`, `webhook`, `database` or `agent` when they show up in technical conversations.

This glossary gives you that foundation in simple Spanish.

## What is included

- Concepts for understanding what you can build: websites, apps, bots, APIs, agents, and internal systems.
- Publishing vocabulary: servers, environments, production, environment variables, and scheduled tasks.
- Internet terms: domains, URLs, requests, APIs, headers, and webhooks.
- Technical basics around code, Git, databases, and automations.
- Core AI concepts: LLMs, prompts, tokens, embeddings, RAG, agents, and tools.
- Real phrases you will hear when working with systems, developers, or AI assistants.

## Who is it for?

Entrepreneurs, creators, and operators who want to use AI to build real things but still get stuck on technical vocabulary.

It also works as a quick reference before asking ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor, or any other LLM for help.

## How to use it

Read it once end to end and keep it as a reference. When an LLM uses a word you do not recognize, return to the glossary and rewrite your request more precisely.
